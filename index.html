What is WBTC

What a WBTC bridge does

WBTC is Wrapped Bitcoin, an ERC-20 token on Ethereum that is backed 1:1 by BTC held by a custodian network. A WBTC bridge moves that token from one EVM chain to another, for example from Ethereum to Arbitrum, Optimism, Base or Polygon.

Moving the token, not the Bitcoin

Bridging changes which chain your WBTC sits on. It does not touch the underlying BTC and does not create new WBTC against real bitcoin. That is minting, and it is handled by merchants and the custodian network, not by this interface.

What you receive on the other chain

Depending on the bridge protocol and the route, WBTC on a Layer 2 may be a bridged representation rather than the canonical Ethereum token. Check the token contract on the destination chain before you rely on it.

Bridge vs mint

Bridging is not minting

Two different actions are easy to confuse. One moves WBTC you already hold between chains. The other turns real BTC into WBTC, or back, through the custodian side.

In practice

If you already hold WBTC and want it on another EVM network, you bridge. If you hold BTC and want WBTC, or the reverse, you go through a merchant or the custodian, which is outside this interface.

How to bridge

How to bridge WBTC

Route and quote

The interface asks bridge protocols for routes between your source and destination chain. Each quote shows the expected amount received, the bridge fee and the estimated time, and nothing is signed until you confirm.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and pick the source chain and the destination chain, such as Ethereum to Arbitrum.
  2. Choose WBTC and enter an amount, then review the route, fee and estimated arrival.
  3. Approve WBTC for the bridge contract if this is your first bridge of it from this wallet.
  4. Sign the bridge transaction on the source chain.
  5. Wait for the transfer to arrive, then check the WBTC contract address on the destination chain.

Some routes take minutes and others take longer. Native optimistic rollup withdrawals back to Ethereum use a challenge window of about 7 days.

Fees & gas

What bridging WBTC costs

Gas on each chain

You pay gas on the chain where you sign. That is ETH on Ethereum and on most L2s such as Arbitrum, Optimism and Base, and POL on Polygon. See how gas works.

Bridge fee

Bridge protocols may charge a fee or build their cost into the rate. It is included in the quote, so compare the amount received rather than only the fee line.

Keep native tokens on both sides

Holding WBTC does not pay for gas. Keep some native token on the source chain to sign, and on the destination chain so you can move or use the WBTC after it arrives.

Security

Staying safe when you bridge

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. This interface requests quotes and builds transactions. It never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify the WBTC contract address on the destination chain, since bridged versions can differ from the canonical token.
  • Approve only the amount you need, and revoke old allowances you no longer use.
  • Check the destination chain, recipient address and quote before confirming.
  • Bridges add smart contract and validator risk on top of WBTC's custodian risk, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

WBTC Bridge FAQ

What is a WBTC bridge?

A WBTC bridge moves Wrapped Bitcoin from one EVM chain to another, such as Ethereum to Arbitrum or Base. It transfers the token across networks and does not convert it to or from real BTC.

Is bridging WBTC the same as minting it?

No. Bridging moves WBTC you already hold between chains. Minting and redeeming turn BTC into WBTC and back, and that is done by merchants and the custodian network, not by this interface.

Is WBTC on Arbitrum or Base the same token as on Ethereum?

Not always. Depending on the bridge, WBTC on a Layer 2 may be a bridged representation rather than the canonical Ethereum token. Verify the contract address on the destination chain before using it.

Which chains can I bridge WBTC between?

Ethereum and other EVM networks such as Arbitrum, Optimism, Base and Polygon, through bridge protocols. Available routes depend on the protocol and are shown in the quote.

What do I pay gas in when bridging WBTC?

Gas is paid in the native token of the chain you sign on. That is ETH on Ethereum and most L2s, and POL on Polygon. Keep a small balance on both the source and destination chain.

Does this interface hold my WBTC?

No. It is non-custodial. You connect your own wallet and sign each approval and bridge transaction, and the interface never has access to your keys or funds.
